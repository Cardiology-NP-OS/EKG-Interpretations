# PKT-EP1-08 Pattern Candidate Evidence Contract

This layer is quarantined and non-diagnostic.

- Registry authority is the exact 59-pattern `23_PATTERN_REGISTRY.json`.
- Only patterns explicitly listed in `PKT08_EXECUTABLE_RULES.json` may execute.
- The default for every unencoded registry pattern is `NOT_EXECUTABLE`.
- Required evidence and supportive evidence are never interchangeable.
- Active confounders or unresolved contradictions block a present candidate.
- Registry diagnostic boundaries and urgency metadata are always preserved.
- Urgency does not increase diagnostic certainty.
- Component helpers (rate, QRS duration, Sgarbossa components) never complete a morphology-dependent pattern by themselves.
- `PRESENT_CANDIDATE` is not a diagnosis.
- `candidate_active=false`, `clinical_accuracy_claimed=false`, and `automatic_selection_allowed=false` are invariant.
